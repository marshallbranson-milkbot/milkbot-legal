# MilkBot Privacy Policy

  **Effective Date:** 2026-04-27

  ## What we collect
  MilkBot stores only what's needed to make the game work:

  - Your Discord user ID (numeric — used as a database key)
  - Your in-game state: milk-buck balance, XP, prestige level, owned items, gear, dungeon progress, pets, achievements,
  daily quest progress
  - Per-server context (which guild a balance belongs to)
  - Optional: a referral relationship if you use `/refer`

  ## What we do NOT collect
  - Your real name, email, address, or phone number
  - Discord messages outside of MilkBot's own commands and buttons
  - Your IP address
  - Payment information (handled directly by Discord for premium features — we never see card details)
  - Any data not directly tied to MilkBot gameplay

  ## Where it is stored
  Game state is stored in JSON files on Railway (our hosting provider) and is access-restricted to the bot itself. We
  use atomic writes to prevent data corruption.

  ## Who we share it with
  Nobody. We don't sell, rent, share, or advertise your data. Aggregate, anonymized stats may appear in patch notes
  (e.g., "127 players cleared D8 last week").

  ## Data deletion
  You can request full deletion of your MilkBot data by DMing the bot owner or emailing marshallbranson@gmail.com. We'll
   wipe your record within 14 days.

  ## Children
  You must be 13 or older to use Discord (and therefore MilkBot) per Discord's Terms of Service.

  ## Changes
  We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. The Effective Date at the top will reflect the most recent change.
